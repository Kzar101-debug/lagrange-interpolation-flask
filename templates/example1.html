Example 1

Given: (1,2), (2,4), (3,8), find f(2.5)

$$P(x)=y_0L_0(x)+y_1L_1(x)+y_2L_2(x)$$ $$L_0(2.5)=\frac{(2.5-2)(2.5-3)}{(1-2)(1-3)}=0.125$$ $$L_1(2.5)=\frac{(2.5-1)(2.5-3)}{(2-1)(2-3)}=0.75$$ $$L_2(2.5)=\frac{(2.5-1)(2.5-2)}{(3-1)(3-2)}=0.375$$ $$P(2.5)=2(0.125)+4(0.75)+8(0.375)$$ $$P(2.5)=6.25$$

Final Answer: 6.25
