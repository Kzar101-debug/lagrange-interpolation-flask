Given: (1,2), (2,4), (3,8), find f(2.5)

Step 1: Formula

$$P(x)=y_0L_0(x)+y_1L_1(x)+y_2L_2(x)$$

Step 2: Compute L₀(2.5)

$$L_0(2.5)=\frac{(2.5-2)(2.5-3)}{(1-2)(1-3)}=0.125$$

Step 3: Compute L₁(2.5)

$$L_1(2.5)=\frac{(2.5-1)(2.5-3)}{(2-1)(2-3)}=0.75$$

Step 4: Compute L₂(2.5)

$$L_2(2.5)=\frac{(2.5-1)(2.5-2)}{(3-1)(3-2)}=0.375$$

Step 5: Final Answer

$$P(2.5)=2(0.125)+4(0.75)+8(0.375)=6.25$$
